Common Errors
OA Switchboard:
  • couldn't find or improperly formatted orcid
  • no matching license
    • cannot find the license of this article in our figshare account. it should be added and then retried
  • missing fields
    • probably shouldn't bother doing anything here. this is a mistake on OA Switchboards end
Figshare:
  • There can't be 2 users having the same orcid
    • This happens when trying to upload the article to figshare - it may be that the orcid exists already on the figshare but the name is different.
